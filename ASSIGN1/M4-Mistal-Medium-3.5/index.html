How we work

Embedded. Iterative. Done.

The forward-deployed method: live where the problem lives, ship small, measure everything.

Dive in

A senior FDE embeds with your team for an intensive discovery — shadowing operators, auditing systems, and finding the real bottleneck.

Blueprint

You get a concrete plan in days, not months: solution architecture, success metrics, and a fixed-scope first milestone.

Ship weekly

We build and deploy in weekly increments against real data. You see working software from week one — no big-bang reveals.

Hand over

Documentation, training, and a maintainable codebase. We stay on call through stabilization, then step back.
