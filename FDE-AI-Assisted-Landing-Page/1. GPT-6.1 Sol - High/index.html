What is a forward deployed engineer?

A forward deployed engineer works directly with your team and your users. They combine hands-on engineering with an understanding of your business to build solutions in the environment where they’ll actually be used.

How is this different from an agency?

We work inside your delivery process, with shared goals and direct collaboration. Instead of passing requirements back and forth, we help define the problem, build the solution, and transfer knowledge as we go.

Can you work with our existing tech stack?

Yes. We begin by understanding your architecture, constraints, and team’s strengths. Together, we agree on the right scope and technical fit before starting an engagement.

What does an engagement look like?

We start with a focused discovery conversation, agree on outcomes and scope, then work in short delivery cycles. The team structure and timeline depend on what you need to achieve.

Who owns the code and deliverables?

You do. The engagement is designed to leave you with your source code, documentation, and the knowledge to operate and extend the solution. Ownership terms are agreed before work begins.
