## Final Verdict

When I was reading Opus result, it was much more clearer on what information it was delivering. I clearly understood that it is an FDE service provider. The engagements section and faq section were excellently done. Its use of text size, text bold, text color shade to make a UX friendly website was done on point.

On the other hand even though I understood what DeepSeek was trying to deliver with its information however, it felt a bit off. It seemed like I understood it because of my engineering background. The engagement section was confusing, mentioning the price felt like it was a tiered based service instead of providing 3 different services. Adding a faq section would have been better as well.

Overall I give Opus the win.
