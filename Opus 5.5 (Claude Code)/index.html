Process

From first call to production in four steps

  1. Week 0

    Scope

    We agree one problem, one owner, and one metric. You get a written plan and a fixed pilot price.

  2. Weeks 1–2

    Embed

    Engineers join your team, learn the workflow first-hand, and get a prototype running on real data.

  3. Weeks 3–6

    Ship

    Weekly releases to real users. Feedback from the floor goes straight into the next build.

  4. Weeks 7–8

    Hand over

    We harden, document, and train. You decide whether to extend, expand, or run it yourselves.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the first call

How is this different from consulting or staff augmentation?

Consultants hand over recommendations and contractors take tickets. Forward deployed engineers own a business outcome, find the problem themselves, and write the production code that fixes it.

Do your engineers work on-site?

Yes, wherever it helps. Most engagements start with time on-site next to the people doing the work, then settle into a mix of on-site and remote.

Who owns the code?

You do. Everything is built in your repositories and environments, and all IP transfers to you.

How do you handle security and data access?

We work inside your environment under your access controls, sign your NDA and data agreements, and follow your review and deployment processes.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You get documentation, runbooks, and a trained team. You can extend the engagement, move to light-touch support, or run everything on your own.
