Process

    Scope

    Embed

  3. Week 2–6

FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

An FDE is a senior software engineer who works directly with your customers rather than on your core product. They write production code in the customer's environment to integrate, configure, and extend your platform for that customer's needs.

Do your engineers represent our brand?

Yes. FDEs operate under your company name, use your email domain if you prefer, and follow your customer communication standards.

How quickly can an engineer start?

Most engagements start within 5–10 business days of a signed scope, depending on security clearance and access requirements.

Who owns the code we build?

You do, or your customer does, as agreed in the scope. We assign all IP created during the engagement.

Have a deal stuck in pilot?

Tell us about the account. We'll reply within one business day with a proposed scope and an engineer match.
